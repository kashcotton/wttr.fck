wttr.fck

Asking the sky what its problem is…

A free weather app with attitude

wttr.fck gives you the real forecast for anywhere in the world (current conditions, hourly and 10-day forecasts, air quality, UV, wind and sunrise times) and then roasts you about it.

  • Three language levels: Clean, Salty and Unhinged
  • Voices: Classic Jerk, Cable News Pundit, Disappointed Parent, Middle Manager, Campaign Ad, Conspiracy Uncle
  • Optional political satire that roasts every side equally: the Pundit Panel and Election Night
  • Misery index, "should I go outside?" verdict and what to wear
  • Community written: anyone can add roasts on GitHub
  • Free, no ads, no account, open source. Weather data by Open-Meteo.com
